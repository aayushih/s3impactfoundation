## Who we are

S3 Impact Foundation is a charitable foundation based at 2nd Floor, Chandramani Apartments, Plot No. 133/134, Road No. 3, Matunga Central, Mumbai 400019. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean S3 Impact Foundation.

## What we collect

When you write to us through the contact form, we receive your name, your organisation (if you share it), your email address, the topic you choose and your message.

We do not ask for sensitive personal information through this website, and we do not sell, rent or trade the information you give us.

## How we use it

We use what you send only to reply to you, to follow up on the partnership, project or visit you asked about, and to keep a record of our correspondence.

## Where it is kept

Messages sent through the form are delivered by our website host, Netlify, and kept in our email. We keep them only for as long as we need them for the purposes above.

## Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising cookies. If we add a privacy-friendly visitor count in future, we will say so here.
