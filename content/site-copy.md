### Our Inspiration **[CONFIRM]**
**Heading:** Why S3
Real change is never only material. A family needs food, water, schooling and work. It also needs dignity, purpose and a sense of belonging.

Our name holds these together. S3 stands for three commitments: **Social Impact**, for the people we serve; **Sustainability**, for the land that sustains them; and **Self Transformation**, for the inner growth that makes every other kind of change last.

### Mission & Vision
**Our mission:** To invest in people, strengthen communities and restore the land, so that progress on the outside is matched by growth on the inside.

**Our vision:** Villages where every child learns, every family eats well, every pond runs clean, and every person has room to grow in spirit.

### Our approach
**Heading:** How we work
- **We fund, and we follow through.** We support trusted partners on the ground and stay close to the work from first survey to final report.
- **We measure what matters.** Water is lab-tested, learning is tracked from baseline to endline, and harvests are logged every month.
- **We start where people are.** Many of our projects begin in the villages around Govardhan Ecovillage, where we can see needs clearly and respond quickly.

### Our Values (values wheel) **[CONFIRM]**
**Centre text:** S3
**Ring text:** Who we serve · How we work · What we stand for

- **Evidence over assumption.** We test, measure and report, and we say what has not worked yet.
- **Local hands, local pride.** Villagers, farmers and "friends of the kund" are partners, not beneficiaries.
- **Care for the whole person.** Body, mind and spirit, especially for those who are most vulnerable.
- **Stewardship.** Of land, water, heritage and every rupee entrusted to us.

## 3. Our Work

### Page intro
**Heading:** Our Work
**Text:** S3 Impact Foundation invests in projects that serve people, communities and the land: from food and farming to education, water, health, elder care and culture. Here are the projects the Foundation has carried out, and those it is preparing to support next.
(From the Drive Projects Page.)

### Rural Development at GEV
**Intro:** Palghar district, about 100 km north of Mumbai, is largely tribal. Farming is hard, work is seasonal, and schools and clinics can be far away. From Govardhan Ecovillage, we fund work that meets these needs where people live.

- **Farmers & Agriculture:** Orchards for small farmers, a farm for the community kitchens, and a pilot that grows food and solar power on the same acre.
- **Water Resource Development:** Safe drinking water today, and more monsoon rain stored for tomorrow.
- **Women Empowerment:** Skills that let women earn close to home, and a stronger say at home.
- **Skill Development:** Hands-on training for jobs that are growing, from electric vehicles to tailoring.
- **Rural Education:** Better learning in tribal residential schools, and a new English-medium school on the way.
- **Healthcare & Elder Care:** A caring home for senior devotees, and a masterplan for a 100-bed hospital.
- **Animal Welfare:** A planned sanctuary to vaccinate, sterilise and care for community dogs.

### Govardhan Annakshetra
**Intro:** One kitchen, six meal programmes and 2.4 million free meals in 2025-26, for villagers, schoolchildren, trainees and hospital patients.

### Spiritual & Culture
**Intro:** Some places hold a community's memory. We help restore them and pass their values on: a temple and cultural centre that welcomed over eight lakh visitors in 2024-25, a heritage home in Kolkata, and a values curriculum for children.

### Braj Kunds
**Intro:** The kunds of Braj are sacred ponds, used by villagers and by pilgrims walking the parikrama. A survey of 16 found that about 70% held water unfit even for bathing. We are reviving six of them: Priya Kund, Prem Sarovar, Jal Vihar Kund, Kamal Kund, Vihval Kund and Pavan Sarovar.

### Beyond GEV
**Intro:** Our support reaches past Palghar, to a permanent campus for nearly 300 students in Pune and a charitable hospital caring for pilgrims and village families in Vrindavan.

### Policy & Advocacy
**Intro:** Grassroots work earns a place at bigger tables. Projects we support have helped lead a G20 civil-society working group on hunger and joined a national festival of good governance.

### Stories of Life Transformation
**Heading:** Behind the numbers

**Home, not the work site.** Two farmers from Sukasale village had left home for daily-wage work. Each planted 20 mango, 20 cashew and 10 guava saplings. Both now tend orchards of their own. For one of them, it was the first support he had ever received.
*Link:* Fruit Orchards for Palghar's Small Farmers

**A stronger say at home.** Within three months of training, 65 of 80 women were earning. They report more say in household decisions, and an alumni network now links each new batch to those who came before.
*Link:* Skills and Income for Rural and Tribal Women

**One less worry.** For about 200 patients and carers a day at Bhaktivedanta Hospital, a free meal eases the cost and stress of treatment.
*Link:* Govardhan Annakshetra

**From classroom to career.** Fifty-five young people learned to build electric vehicles. Some were placed with a manufacturer in Pune soon after the course.
*Link:* Electric Vehicle Skilling Course
