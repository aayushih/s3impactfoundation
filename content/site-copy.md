## 2. About Us

### Page intro
**Heading:** About *S3*
**Text:** S3 Impact Foundation is a charitable foundation based in Mumbai. We serve people, communities and the land, and we stay beside each project long enough to see the lives it changes.

### Our Inspiration **[CONFIRM]**
**Heading:** Why *S3*
True change is never only material. A family needs food, water, schooling and work. It also needs dignity, purpose and a sense of belonging.

Our name holds these together. S3 stands for three commitments: **Social Impact**, for the people we serve; **Sustainability**, for the land that sustains them; and **Self Transformation**, for the inner growth that makes every other change last.

### Vision & Mission
**Our Vision**
A world where no one goes hungry, every child can learn, the land is cared for, and every person has the chance to grow in spirit.

**Our Mission**
To nourish people, uplift communities and care for the land, so that every life we touch can grow in spirit as well as in strength.

### How we serve
- **We give, and we stay.** We support trusted partners on the ground and walk with each project from the first survey to the final report.
- **We see the results.** Water is lab-tested, learning is tracked from baseline to endline, and harvests are recorded every month.
- **We begin close to home.** Many of our projects start in the villages around Govardhan Ecovillage, where we can see needs clearly and respond with care.

### Our Values (values wheel) **[CONFIRM]**
**Centre text:** S3
**Ring text:** Who we serve · How we work · What we stand for

- **Local hands, local pride.** Villagers, farmers and "friends of the kund" are partners in the work, not just its beneficiaries.
- **Honest results.** We test, measure and share what we find, including what is still in progress.
- **Stewardship.** Of land, water, heritage and every rupee entrusted to us.

## 3. Our Work

### Page intro
**Heading:** Our *Work*
**Text:** S3 Impact Foundation invests in projects that serve people, communities and the land: from food and farming to education, water, health, elder care and culture. Here are the projects the Foundation has carried out, and those it is preparing to support next.
(From the Drive Projects Page.)

### Rural Development at GEV
**Intro:** Palghar district, about 100 km north of Mumbai, is largely tribal. Farming is hard, work is seasonal, and schools and clinics can be far away. From Govardhan Ecovillage, we serve these villages where people live.

- **Farmers & Agriculture:** Orchards for small farmers, a farm for the community kitchens, and a pilot that grows food and solar power on the same acre.
- **Water Resource Development:** Safe drinking water today, and more monsoon rain saved for tomorrow.
- **Women Empowerment:** Skills that let women earn close to home, and a stronger voice at home.
- **Skill Development:** Hands-on training for growing fields, from electric vehicles to tailoring.
- **Rural Education:** Better learning in tribal residential schools, and a new English-medium school on the way.
- **Healthcare & Elder Care:** A caring home for senior devotees, and a masterplan for a 100-bed hospital.
- **Animal Welfare:** A planned sanctuary to vaccinate, sterilise and care for community dogs.

### Govardhan Annakshetra
**Intro:** One kitchen, six meal programmes and 2.4 million free meals in 2025-26, for villagers, schoolchildren, trainees and hospital patients.

### Spiritual & Culture
**Intro:** Some places hold a community's heart. We help restore them and pass their values on: a temple and cultural centre that welcomed over eight lakh visitors in 2024-25, a heritage home in Kolkata, and a values curriculum for children.

### Braj Kunds
**Intro:** The kunds of Braj are sacred ponds, used by villagers and by pilgrims walking the parikrama. A survey of 16 found that about 70% held water unfit even for bathing. Six are now being revived: Priya Kund, Prem Sarovar, Jal Vihar Kund, Kamal Kund, Vihval Kund and Pavan Sarovar.
**[PLACEHOLDER]** photos for Jal Vihar, Kamal and Vihval Kund.

### Beyond GEV
**Intro:** Our care reaches past Palghar, to a permanent campus for nearly 300 students in Pune and a charitable hospital caring for pilgrims and village families in Vrindavan.

### Policy & Advocacy
**Intro:** Grassroots work earns a place at bigger tables. Projects we support have helped lead a G20 civil-society working group on hunger and joined a national festival of good governance.

### Stories of Life Transformation
**Heading:** Lives behind the *numbers*

**Home, not the work site.** Two farmers from Sukasale village had left home for daily-wage work. Each planted 20 mango, 20 cashew and 10 guava saplings. Both now tend orchards of their own. For one of them, it was the first support he had ever received.
*Link:* Fruit Orchards for Palghar's Small Farmers

**A stronger voice at home.** Within three months of training, 65 of 80 women were earning. They report more say in household decisions, and an alumni network now links each new batch to those who came before.
*Link:* Skills and Income for Rural and Tribal Women

**One less worry.** For about 200 patients and carers a day at Bhaktivedanta Hospital, a free meal eases the cost and stress of treatment.
*Link:* Govardhan Annakshetra

**From classroom to career.** Fifty-five young people learned to build electric vehicles. Some were placed with a manufacturer in Pune soon after the course.
*Link:* Electric Vehicle Skilling Course
